\chapter{Introduction}

\section{Purpose}
This Software Requirements Specification (SRS) defines what \textbf{TrustBazaar} must do and the qualities it must have. TrustBazaar is a web-based, peer-to-peer marketplace in which identity-verified individuals in Bangladesh sell used goods and game accounts to one another, either at a fixed price or through a time-boxed auction. Every payment is held in escrow until the buyer has received what they paid for, delivery is arranged either as a safe in-person handover or through a courier, and disputes are decided while the money is still held.

The SRS is the single reference for the system's scope and required behaviour during design, implementation, testing and academic evaluation. It states \emph{what} the system shall do, not \emph{how} it is built: the architecture, component design, data schema and sequence diagrams belong to the System Design Document (SDD), which will be written after this specification is agreed. The reasons behind the requirements, drawn from a study of Bikroy, Facebook Marketplace and Daraz, are given in the Supplementary Market Analysis (``the Supplementary Report''), and the project plan is given in the revised Project Proposal.

\section{Document Conventions}

\paragraph{Requirement identifiers.} Each requirement has a stable identifier made of a type and an area code, for example \textbf{FR-PAY-07} (functional, payment and escrow), \textbf{SEC-AUTH-03} (security, authentication) or \textbf{NFR-PERF-02} (other non-functional, performance). Identifiers are never reused; a withdrawn requirement keeps its identifier and is marked as withdrawn. Version 1.1 used a single running number (FR-1 to FR-103, NFR-1 to NFR-23); Appendix~\ref{app:map} maps every old number to its new identifier.

\paragraph{Keywords.} \textbf{shall} marks a mandatory requirement; \textbf{should} marks a recommended one whose omission must be justified; \textbf{may} marks an option. Each requirement states one testable behaviour.

\paragraph{Priority (Pri column).} MoSCoW: \textbf{M} Must Have (required for the capstone deliverable), \textbf{S} Should Have (planned; may be descoped with the reason documented), \textbf{C} Could Have (built if time allows), \textbf{W} Won't Have in this release (recorded so the boundary is explicit). A requirement marked \textbf{(prod)} applies to a production deployment only; in the academic deliverable it is met by documentation or by a sandbox equivalent, as the requirement states.

\paragraph{Verification method (Ver column).} \textbf{T} Test (automated or scripted), \textbf{D} Demonstration, \textbf{I} Inspection of code, configuration or documents, \textbf{A} Analysis (calculation, review or measurement report).

\paragraph{Configurable parameters.} Every time limit, threshold and rate used by a requirement is a named parameter, written \textbf{CP-nn}, with its default value listed in Table~\ref{tab:params} (Chapter~\ref{ch:params}). Requirements refer to the parameter, not to a hard-coded number.

\paragraph{Money and time.} Amounts are in Bangladeshi Taka (BDT). Internally, amounts are integers in poisha (1~BDT = 100~poisha), so no amount is ever stored or calculated in floating point. Times are recorded in UTC and shown to users in Bangladesh Standard Time (Asia/Dhaka, UTC+06:00).

\section{Product Scope}
TrustBazaar is a single responsive web application, not a native mobile app, for individual-to-individual resale within Bangladesh. It exists to solve five connected problems that informal channels leave open (Supplementary Report, Sections~3--5):
\begin{enumerate}[nosep]
    \item \textbf{Trust between strangers}: every person who lists, bids, buys or receives money is identity-verified (KYC), and one person can hold only one verified account.
    \item \textbf{Fair price discovery}: a server-timed auction with proxy bidding, a reserve price and anti-sniping runs alongside fixed-price sale and stays correct under simultaneous bidding.
    \item \textbf{Protected payment}: every payment is held in escrow and leaves it only by buyer confirmation after handover, by a timeout counted from a recorded handover, or by an operator's dispute decision.
    \item \textbf{Safe fulfilment}: no cash changes hands at a meeting; the buyer either collects the item at a suggested safe public meetup point or receives it by courier at a system-quoted fare.
    \item \textbf{Safe game-account trading}: ownership is proven before listing, login details are encrypted so that only the buyer can read them, and the payment is held through a protection period after the buyer takes the account over.
\end{enumerate}
Chat, ratings, notifications and monetization support this transaction lifecycle; they are not separate products.

\subsection*{Out of scope}
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
    \item Sale of new or branded retail stock by businesses; TrustBazaar is not a general e-commerce storefront.
    \item A general administration or management console. The internal roles (Platform Operator and Administrator, Section~\ref{sec:classes}) can perform only the specific actions listed in Section~\ref{sec:fr-ops}.
    \item Integration with game publishers. TrustBazaar cannot prevent a publisher from acting against an account that has changed hands; this risk is disclosed to the buyer before payment (\rr{FR-GAM-05}).
    \item Real money movement and live courier bookings in the academic deliverable. Sandbox and test-mode integrations are used, and every substitution is disclosed rather than presented as live.
    \item Native mobile apps, cash on delivery, cryptocurrency payments, international shipping and sales outside Bangladesh.
\end{itemize}

\chapter{Overall Description}

\section{Product Perspective}
TrustBazaar is a new, self-contained system. It replaces no existing system; it competes with Bikroy, Facebook Marketplace groups and informal ``admin deals'', which connect buyers and sellers but leave payment, handover and disputes to the parties (Supplementary Report, Sections~3 and~5). The system interacts with the following external parties; how it is divided internally is a design matter for the SDD.
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
    \item \textbf{Users} through a web browser on a phone or computer.
    \item \textbf{Internal staff} (operators and administrators) through restricted views of the same web application.
    \item \textbf{Payment providers}: bKash, Nagad and Stripe, for collecting payments, refunds and seller payouts.
    \item \textbf{Courier providers}: Pathao or Steadfast through their sandbox APIs, or a documented mock courier service.
    \item \textbf{Messaging providers}: an SMS gateway for OTPs and security alerts, and an e-mail service for transactional messages.
    \item \textbf{Browser platform services}: camera access for the KYC selfie, geolocation for approximate location, and the Web Cryptography API for game-account credentials.
\end{itemize}

\section{Product Functions}
At the highest level, the system shall allow users to:
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
    \item register, verify their phone and e-mail, sign in securely and complete KYC verification;
    \item list a physical item or a game account for fixed-price sale or auction, and browse, search and filter listings;
    \item bid in a server-timed auction or buy a fixed-price item, and chat with the counterparty about a listing;
    \item pay by bKash, Nagad or card into escrow, and receive the item at a safe meetup point or by courier, or receive a game account through a sealed handover and guided takeover;
    \item confirm receipt to release payment, or open a dispute while the payment is still held;
    \item receive payouts to a verified payout account, rate the counterparty, and report fraud;
    \item for sellers, optionally feature a listing or subscribe to the Pro Seller tier.
\end{itemize}
Internal staff can review KYC submissions, ownership proofs, reports and disputes, and manage configuration, each within the limits of Section~\ref{sec:fr-ops}.

\section{User Classes and Characteristics}
\label{sec:classes}
\begin{deftable}
\textbf{Guest} & Not signed in. May browse and search active listings and view public profiles. Cannot chat, bid, buy or list. \\
\textbf{Registered user} & Account with verified phone number but KYC not yet \texttt{verified} (statuses \texttt{unsubmitted}, \texttt{pending\_review}, \texttt{rejected} or \texttt{revoked}). May browse, save searches and chat. Cannot list, bid, buy or receive payouts. \\
\textbf{Verified user} & KYC status \texttt{verified}, aged 18 or over. May act as buyer and seller of physical items and game accounts. The default class for transacting users. \\
\textbf{Pro Seller} & Verified user with an active paid subscription: lower commission, higher listing limit and a sales analytics view (Could Have). \\
\textbf{Platform Operator} & Internal staff. Works the KYC, ownership-proof, report, flagged-activity and dispute queues. Can perform only the actions in \rr{FR-OPS-02}; cannot change configuration or manage staff accounts. MFA is mandatory. \\
\textbf{Administrator} & Internal staff. Manages operator accounts, configurable parameters and feature switches. Cannot view KYC documents, chats or dispute evidence and cannot decide cases. MFA is mandatory. \\
\textbf{External providers} & Payment, courier and messaging providers. Not users of the interface; they interact through authenticated APIs and webhooks. \\
\end{deftable}
Users are assumed to be familiar with smartphones and mobile financial services, but not with auctions or escrow; the interface must explain both in plain language (\rr{NFR-USE-01}, \rr{NFR-USE-07}). Many users will reach the site on mid-range Android phones over mobile data.

\section{Operating Environment}
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
    \item \textbf{Client}: current versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge, on Android, iOS and desktop, with WebSocket, camera (Media Capture) and Web Cryptography API support. Minimum supported viewport width: 360 CSS pixels.
    \item \textbf{Server}: Linux containers run with Docker Compose: PostgreSQL 15, Redis 7, the backend (Node.js 20, NestJS 10, TypeScript 5, with its Socket.IO 4 real-time gateway) and the web client (React 18, React Router 6, built with Vite).
    \item \textbf{Network}: all traffic over HTTPS and secure WebSockets; the system must remain usable on 4G mobile connections typical in Bangladesh.
\end{itemize}

\section{Design and Implementation Constraints}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{C-\arabic*}, nosep, leftmargin=*]
    \item The technology stack is fixed by the project repository [R17]: TypeScript on Node.js 20 with NestJS 10, PostgreSQL 15 accessed through TypeORM, Redis 7 accessed through ioredis, Socket.IO 4, React 18, Docker Compose, Jest, ESLint and GitHub Actions. A change requires agreement of the whole team and an update to this SRS.
    \item Payment integrations shall use official sandbox or test-mode credentials only; no real financial transaction shall be processed in the academic deliverable.
    \item Courier integration shall use a real courier's sandbox API where one is available; otherwise a mock courier that reproduces realistic fares and status updates shall be used, and the substitution shall be labelled in the user interface and documentation.
    \item No custom cryptographic algorithm or protocol shall be written. Only vetted library or platform implementations (Node.js \texttt{crypto}, the browser Web Cryptography API, established password-hashing libraries) shall be used.
    \item The Operator and Administrator roles shall be limited to the actions listed in Section~\ref{sec:fr-ops}; no generic create, edit or delete access to listings, users or orders shall be provided, so that the system remains a marketplace and not a management system.
    \item TrustBazaar shall not integrate with game publishers. Because most publishers prohibit account transfers (Supplementary Report, Section~5.2), the game-account feature shall be switchable off by configuration (\rr{FR-GAM-01}).
    \item \textbf{(prod)} In a production deployment, buyers' funds shall be held through a payment service provider or bank licensed by Bangladesh Bank for escrow or payment services, not in an account controlled solely by TrustBazaar. In the academic deliverable, escrow is simulated by internal state over sandbox payments.
    \item \textbf{(prod)} Personal data classified as Restricted or Confidential (Section~\ref{sec:classification}) shall be stored and processed in a manner compliant with the PDPO [R10], including any requirement to keep such data within Bangladesh.
\end{enumerate}

\section{User Documentation}
The deliverables are this SRS, the System Design Document, a README with setup instructions for Docker Compose, a privacy notice and terms of use, and in-app help. In-app help shall cover at least: the KYC process; auction rules (minimum increment, proxy bidding, reserve price, anti-sniping); how escrow, the handover code and the release timeout work; how to open a dispute; and, for game accounts, the ownership proof, the takeover checklist, the protection period and the publisher-terms risk.

\section{Assumptions and Dependencies}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{AS-\arabic*}, nosep, leftmargin=*]
    \item Sandbox access to bKash and Nagad can be obtained. If it cannot, Stripe test mode alone is used and the limitation is documented (risk accepted in the proposal).
    \item Server clocks are synchronised by NTP; auction fairness and every deadline in this SRS depend on it.
    \item Courier fares are reasonable approximations of published rate cards, not certified live quotes, unless a live sandbox is available.
    \item An SMS gateway with a test mode, or a logged mock that is labelled as such, is available for OTP delivery.
    \item Users' browsers support the Web Cryptography API; a browser without it cannot take part in a game-account sale and is told so before purchase.
    \item The protection given to game-account credentials assumes that the code the server sends to browsers has not been tampered with. It protects against database and backup compromise and against operators; it does not protect against an attacker who can change the deployed application code. That residual risk is reduced by the change controls in Section~\ref{sec:sec-sdl}.
    \item KYC verification by an operator from an NID image and a selfie raises the cost of fraud but does not make it impossible; fake NIDs exist (Supplementary Report, Section~5.2). The design relies on escrow and identity checks covering each other's gaps.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Release Scope}
The capstone deliverable comprises all Must Have requirements. Should Have and Could Have requirements are descoped in the order given in the proposal (delivery automation, notifications beyond those required for security, featured listings, Pro Seller tier) if the schedule slips. Requirements marked W are deliberately excluded, including: a paid buyer-protection add-on (protection is the same for every buyer); cash on delivery; and automatic approval of KYC without an operator.

\section{Account Registration and Authentication (ACC)}
\begin{reqs}
\req{FR-ACC-01}{M}{T}{FR-1}{The system shall allow a person to register with a Bangladeshi mobile number, an e-mail address, a display name and a password that satisfies \rr{SEC-AUTH-01} and \rr{SEC-AUTH-02}.}
\req{FR-ACC-02}{M}{T}{new}{The system shall activate an account only after the mobile number has been verified by OTP (\rr{SEC-AUTH-09}). It shall verify the e-mail address by a single-use link (\cp{CP-06}) before the user's first purchase, listing or payout.}
\req{FR-ACC-03}{M}{T}{new}{The system shall not create a second account for a mobile number or e-mail address already linked to an account. The attempt shall be handled as in \rr{SEC-AUTH-06}, so that it does not reveal which accounts exist.}
\req{FR-ACC-04}{M}{T}{new}{At registration the system shall require the person to confirm they are 18 or older and to accept the current terms of use and privacy notice. It shall record the version of each document accepted and the time.}
\req{FR-ACC-05}{M}{T}{FR-3}{The system shall allow a registered user to sign in with mobile number or e-mail plus password, followed by a second factor where one is enabled (\rr{FR-ACC-06}), and shall then establish a session as specified in Section~\ref{sec:sec-ses}.}
\req{FR-ACC-06}{S}{T}{new}{The system shall allow a user to enable an authenticator-app second factor (TOTP, RFC 6238), shall issue single-use recovery codes when it is enabled, and shall require a current second factor to disable it. MFA is mandatory for staff accounts (\rr{SEC-AUTH-07}).}
\req{FR-ACC-07}{M}{T}{new}{The system shall allow a user to reset a forgotten password through a single-use link sent to the verified e-mail, or an OTP sent to the verified mobile number, under the rules in \rr{SEC-AUTH-10}.}
\req{FR-ACC-08}{M}{T}{new}{The system shall allow a user to view their active sessions (device type, approximate location, last activity), end any one of them, and end all sessions except the current one.}
\req{FR-ACC-09}{M}{T}{new}{The system shall allow a user to change their mobile number or e-mail only after step-up authentication (\rr{SEC-AUTH-08}) and verification of the new value. It shall notify the old value of the change.}
\req{FR-ACC-10}{M}{T}{new}{The system shall show other users only a user's display name, verification badge, rating summary, number of completed transactions, join month and approximate area (district). It shall never show another user's mobile number, e-mail address, NID details or exact location.}
\req{FR-ACC-11}{S}{T}{new}{The system shall allow a user to request closure of their account. Closure shall be refused while the user has an open order, an unpaid auction win, an open dispute or an unpaid balance owed to the platform. After closure the user's data shall be handled as in Table~\ref{tab:retention}.}
\end{reqs}

\section{Identity Verification (KYC)}
\begin{reqs}
\req{FR-KYC-01}{M}{T}{FR-4}{The system shall require a registered user to submit, for KYC: images of the front and back of their NID (or the photo page of a passport); the document number, full name and date of birth as printed; and a live selfie.}
\req{FR-KYC-02}{M}{D}{new}{The system shall capture the selfie through the device camera in the browser and shall not accept an uploaded file in its place. It shall ask the user to perform one randomly chosen action (for example, turning the head or blinking) during capture, as a basic liveness check.}
\req{FR-KYC-03}{M}{T}{new}{The system shall reject, and refer to an operator, any submission whose date of birth shows the person is under 18.}
\req{FR-KYC-04}{M}{T}{new}{The system shall not allow one NID or passport number to belong to more than one account. A submission whose document number matches another account (in any status other than \texttt{unsubmitted}) or the NID blacklist (\rr{FR-TNS-06}) shall be routed to an operator and shall not be approved automatically. The comparison shall use a blind index (\rr{SEC-DAT-03}).}
\req{FR-KYC-05}{M}{T}{FR-6}{The system shall track each user's KYC status as exactly one of \texttt{unsubmitted}, \texttt{pending\_review}, \texttt{verified}, \texttt{rejected} or \texttt{revoked}, and shall record every status change with time, actor and reason.}
\req{FR-KYC-06}{M}{T}{FR-6}{An operator shall reject a submission only with a reason code from a fixed list (for example: image unreadable, selfie does not match, document expired, details do not match image, underage, duplicate identity). The reason shall be shown to the user with guidance on how to fix it.}
\req{FR-KYC-07}{M}{T}{FR-7}{The system shall allow only users whose KYC status is \texttt{verified} to create a listing, place a bid, start a purchase, register a payout account or receive a payout. This shall be enforced server-side on every such request, and users in \texttt{pending\_review} shall be refused as well.}
\req{FR-KYC-08}{M}{T}{FR-8}{The system shall display a Verified badge on the profile and listings of every user whose KYC status is \texttt{verified}, and shall remove it immediately if the status changes.}
\req{FR-KYC-09}{M}{T}{FR-9}{The system shall allow a user whose submission was rejected to resubmit, up to \cp{CP-34}.}
\req{FR-KYC-10}{M}{T}{new}{An operator may revoke a user's verification only with a recorded reason and four-eyes approval (\rr{FR-OPS-05}). On revocation, the system shall hide the user's active listings and block new bids and purchases. Open orders shall continue to completion under the normal escrow rules.}
\req{FR-KYC-11}{C}{T}{proposal AI}{The system should run an automated pre-check on each submission (image legibility and cropping, plausibility of the selfie-to-photo match) using an existing model or API, and attach the result to the review as advisory information. The pre-check shall never approve a submission; it may only flag one for closer review.}
\req{FR-KYC-12}{S}{A}{new}{The system should present each new submission in the operator queue within one minute, and operators should decide it within \cp{CP-44}. The user shall be shown the expected review time.}
\end{reqs}

\section{Listing Creation and Management (LST)}
\begin{reqs}
\req{FR-LST-01}{M}{T}{FR-10, FR-12}{The system shall allow a verified user to create a listing with: title, description, category, item kind (\texttt{physical} or \texttt{game\_account}) and sale format (\texttt{fixed\_price} or \texttt{auction}). Item kind and sale format shall not be changeable after publication.}
\req{FR-LST-02}{M}{T}{FR-10, FR-11}{For a physical item, the system shall require a condition (new, like new, used, for parts) and at least two photos. The seller shall either mark one photo as showing defects or declare that the item has no visible defects; the declaration shall be shown on the listing.}
\req{FR-LST-03}{S}{T}{new}{For a physical item, the system shall require a weight class and a package-size class, for use by the courier fare model (\rr{FR-DLV-07}).}
\req{FR-LST-04}{M}{T}{FR-13}{For a fixed-price listing, the system shall require a price and an available quantity of at least 1.}
\req{FR-LST-05}{M}{T}{FR-14}{For an auction listing, the system shall require a starting price and a duration chosen from \cp{CP-07}. It shall accept an optional reserve price, which shall be at least the starting price. The quantity of an auction listing is always 1.}
\req{FR-LST-06}{M}{T}{FR-15}{For a physical item, the system shall record the seller's approximate pickup location, taken from the listing or from the seller's saved area, for distance calculation. Only the district or area name shall be shown on the listing.}
\req{FR-LST-07}{M}{T}{FR-16}{Before the first bid or order on a listing, the seller may edit its title, description and photos, and, for a fixed-price listing, its price and quantity. After the first bid or order, the seller may only add time-stamped clarifications, shown below the original description. An auction's starting price, reserve price and duration shall never be editable after publication.}
\req{FR-LST-08}{M}{T}{FR-17}{The seller may cancel a fixed-price listing that has no order awaiting payment or fulfilment, or an auction that has no bids. An auction with at least one bid may be cancelled only by an operator, with a recorded reason, and all bidders shall be notified.}
\req{FR-LST-09}{M}{T}{FR-18}{The system shall limit the number of active listings per seller to \cp{CP-28}, with a higher limit for Pro Sellers.}
\req{FR-LST-10}{M}{T}{new}{The system shall maintain a list of prohibited item categories, including at least: weapons and ammunition; drugs and controlled medicines; alcohol and tobacco; stolen goods; counterfeit or replica goods; government-issued documents; SIM cards registered to another person; live animals; adult content; and accounts other than game accounts (for example social-media, banking or MFS accounts). The prohibited categories shall not be selectable when creating a listing.}
\req{FR-LST-11}{S}{T}{new}{The system should screen listing text against a maintained list of prohibited and high-risk keywords (in Bangla and English) and should hold a matching listing in \texttt{pending\_review} for an operator, rather than publishing it.}
\req{FR-LST-12}{M}{T}{new}{The system shall give each listing exactly one status out of \texttt{draft}, \texttt{pending\_review}, \texttt{active}, \texttt{sold}, \texttt{ended\_unsold}, \texttt{cancelled} or \texttt{removed}. It shall record every status change with time, actor and reason.}
\req{FR-LST-13}{C}{D}{proposal AI}{The system may offer to draft a title and description from the uploaded photos using an existing vision-language model or API. The draft shall be shown to the seller for editing and shall never be published without the seller's explicit confirmation.}
\end{reqs}

\req{FR-SRC-02}{M}{T}{FR-20}{The system shall allow results to be filtered by category, item kind, sale format, price range, condition, and maximum distance from the searcher's location or chosen area.}

\section{Auction Engine (AUC)}
\begin{reqs}
\req{FR-AUC-01}{M}{T}{FR-23}{The server shall hold the authoritative end time of every auction. Countdowns shown by the client shall be derived from server time and treated as informational only.}
\req{FR-AUC-02}{M}{T}{FR-24, new}{The system shall accept a bid only if all of the following hold: the bidder is \texttt{verified}, not suspended from bidding, and not the seller; the auction is \texttt{active}; the server receives the bid before the authoritative end time; and the amount is at least the starting price (first bid) or at least the current price plus the minimum increment \cp{CP-08}.}
\req{FR-AUC-03}{M}{T}{FR-25}{The system shall serialise bid processing per auction so that, however many bids arrive at the same moment, each is evaluated against the state left by the previous one, and two bids can never both be recorded as the highest.}
\req{FR-AUC-04}{M}{T}{FR-26}{The system shall reject any bid received after the authoritative end time, even if the client sent it before its local countdown reached zero. Acceptance shall be decided by the time the server receives the bid.}
\req{FR-AUC-05}{M}{T}{FR-27}{When a valid bid is accepted within the anti-sniping window \cp{CP-09} before the current end time, the system shall set the end time to the time the bid was received plus the extension in \cp{CP-09}, and shall broadcast the new end time.}
\req{FR-AUC-06}{M}{T}{FR-28}{The system shall allow a bidder to set a proxy maximum. Whenever that bidder is outbid, it shall automatically place the smallest bid that restores them as highest bidder, never exceeding their maximum.}
\req{FR-AUC-07}{M}{T}{new}{When two proxy maximums are equal, the one set earlier shall win. The current price shall be the lower of the winner's maximum and the second-highest maximum plus the minimum increment.}
\req{FR-AUC-08}{M}{T}{FR-29}{The system shall never reveal a bidder's proxy maximum to anyone else, including the seller and operators, except to an operator investigating a fraud flag on that auction.}
\req{FR-AUC-09}{M}{T}{FR-30}{If a reserve price is set and the highest bid at close is below it, the auction shall end as \texttt{ended\_unsold} and no order shall be created. While the reserve has not been met, the listing shall say ``reserve not met'' without revealing the amount.}
\req{FR-AUC-10}{M}{T}{new}{Bids are binding and cannot be retracted by the bidder. An operator may void a bid only on evidence of fraud or account compromise, with a recorded reason. The system shall then recompute the auction state as if the bid had never been placed, and shall notify affected bidders.}
\req{FR-AUC-11}{M}{T}{FR-31}{The system shall broadcast every change of current price, highest-bidder mask and end time to every client viewing the auction (\rr{NFR-PERF-03}).}
\req{FR-AUC-12}{M}{T}{FR-32}{The system shall notify a bidder in real time when they are outbid (\rr{FR-NTF-01}).}
\req{FR-AUC-13}{M}{T}{new}{The bid history shall be public, showing amounts and times, with each bidder shown by a consistent mask per auction (for example ``Bidder 3''). It shall not identify bidders to the seller, to other bidders or to the public.}
\req{FR-AUC-14}{M}{T}{FR-33}{When an auction closes with a valid winning bid, the system shall create an order for the winner at the winning price and require payment within \cp{CP-10}. It shall notify the winner and the seller.}
\req{FR-AUC-15}{M}{T}{FR-34}{If the winner does not pay within \cp{CP-10}, the system shall cancel that order and offer the item to the next-highest eligible bidder at that bidder's highest bid, to be accepted and paid within \cp{CP-11}. If no eligible bidder accepts, the listing shall end as \texttt{ended\_unsold}, and the seller may relist it.}
\req{FR-AUC-16}{M}{T}{new}{A winner who fails to pay shall receive a non-payment strike. On reaching \cp{CP-12} strikes, the user shall be suspended from bidding for the period in \cp{CP-12}, and told why.}
\req{FR-AUC-17}{M}{T}{FR-88}{The system shall rate-limit bid submissions per user per auction (\cp{CP-35}). This shall not limit bids placed automatically by the proxy mechanism.}
\req{FR-AUC-18}{S}{T}{proposal AI}{The system should score bidding activity for patterns that suggest shill bidding or collusion (for example, many small bids from newly verified accounts, or accounts linked by device or payout account to the seller). It should place flagged auctions in the operator's flagged-activity queue without blocking any bid automatically.}
\end{reqs}

\section{Fixed-Price Purchase (FIX)}
These requirements apply to physical items and game accounts sold at a fixed price.
\begin{reqs}
\req{FR-FIX-01}{M}{T}{FR-35}{The system shall allow a verified buyer, other than the seller, to start a purchase of a fixed-price listing at the listed price, for a quantity not exceeding the quantity available.}
\req{FR-FIX-02}{M}{T}{FR-36}{Starting a purchase shall reserve the quantity and create an order awaiting payment in one atomic operation. Concurrent requests shall never reserve more units than are available.}
\req{FR-FIX-03}{M}{T}{new}{If payment is not confirmed within the checkout hold \cp{CP-13}, the system shall cancel the order and return the reserved quantity to the listing.}
\req{FR-FIX-04}{M}{T}{FR-37}{When the available quantity reaches zero and no reservation remains open, the system shall mark the listing \texttt{sold} and remove it from search results. While reservations are still open it shall show the listing as ``currently reserved''.}
\req{FR-FIX-05}{M}{T}{FR-38}{Under a burst of simultaneous purchase requests, every request shall receive a definite response (reserved, or not available) and none shall be silently dropped. The total reserved shall never exceed the quantity listed.}
\end{reqs}
\section{Payment and Escrow Settlement (PAY)}
\label{sec:fr-pay}
\begin{reqs}
\req{FR-PAY-01}{M}{T}{FR-39}{The system shall accept payment by bKash, Nagad and card, using each provider's sandbox or test mode in the academic deliverable (C-2).}
\req{FR-PAY-02}{M}{T}{FR-40}{The system shall treat a payment as confirmed only on a server-to-server confirmation from the provider (webhook, callback or verification query), never on the client's redirect or on any value sent by the browser.}
\req{FR-PAY-03}{M}{T}{new}{Before confirming a payment, the system shall verify that the provider reports a successful payment whose order reference, amount and currency exactly match the order. On any mismatch it shall not place funds in escrow, shall flag the payment for operator review and shall start a refund through the provider.}
\req{FR-PAY-04}{M}{T}{FR-41}{The system shall process provider confirmations idempotently: a confirmation delivered more than once, or confirmed both by webhook and by query, shall have exactly one effect.}
\req{FR-PAY-05}{M}{T}{FR-42}{On confirmed payment, the system shall place the full amount collected (item price plus any delivery fee) in escrow in state \texttt{held}. No part of it shall be payable to the seller until a release condition in \rr{FR-PAY-07} is met.}
\req{FR-PAY-06}{M}{T}{FR-44}{The escrow of each order shall be in exactly one of the states \texttt{held}, \texttt{frozen\_disputed}, \texttt{released}, \texttt{refunded} or \texttt{partially\_refunded}, and shall change state only by a transition listed in Table~\ref{tab:escrow}. Any other transition shall be rejected and logged as a security event.}
\req{FR-PAY-07}{M}{T}{FR-43}{Escrowed funds shall be released to the seller only by one of the following. (a) The buyer confirms receipt after the delivery event. (b) The auto-release timeout \cp{CP-19} expires after the delivery event, with no dispute open. (c) An operator decides a dispute in the seller's favour. (d) For game accounts only, the rule in \rr{FR-GAM-17} applies. The system shall not offer the buyer a ``confirm receipt'' action before the delivery event.}
\req{FR-PAY-08}{M}{T}{new}{The auto-release timeout shall be counted from the delivery event, never from payment. If the required fulfilment step is not recorded by its deadline, the system shall cancel the order and refund the buyer in full, unless a dispute is open. The steps and deadlines are: the handover for a meetup (\cp{CP-15}); courier pickup (\cp{CP-16}); and credential delivery for a game account (\cp{CP-22}).}
\req{FR-PAY-09}{M}{T}{new}{Before the delivery event, the buyer may request cancellation and the seller may accept it, and the seller may cancel unilaterally; either cancellation shall refund the buyer in full. A unilateral seller cancellation after payment shall count as a seller strike (\rr{FR-TNS-04}).}
\req{FR-PAY-10}{M}{T}{FR-44}{The system shall record every escrow state transition with time, triggering actor (buyer, seller, operator or system timer), reason and the amounts affected, in an append-only history that cannot be edited or deleted through the application.}
\req{FR-PAY-11}{M}{T}{FR-45}{The system shall support full and partial refunds when a dispute is decided in the buyer's favour. In a partial refund, the refunded amount shall be taken from the item price first; platform commission shall be charged only on the part of the item price released to the seller; and the delivery fee shall be refunded only if the courier had not picked up the item.}
\req{FR-PAY-12}{M}{T}{new}{Refunds shall be paid to the original payment method through the provider's refund function. Each refund shall have its own ledger entry and provider reference, and a failed refund shall be retried (\rr{FR-PAY-16}) and shown to an operator.}
\req{FR-PAY-13}{M}{T}{FR-46}{When an order reaches a terminal escrow state, the system shall create one itemised settlement record showing: amount collected, item price, delivery fee, platform commission, courier payout, platform delivery margin, seller payout and any refund.}
\req{FR-PAY-14}{M}{T}{new}{All monetary amounts shall be integers in poisha. Percentages shall be applied with half-up rounding to the nearest poisha, and the seller's payout shall be calculated last as the remainder. In every settlement record, the parts shall add up exactly to the amount collected.}
\req{FR-PAY-15}{M}{T}{FR-47, NFR-10}{A scheduled reconciliation job shall compare every non-terminal order's payment and refund status with the provider's records at least every \cp{CP-46}. It shall correct missed confirmations through the normal idempotent path and report any discrepancy it cannot resolve to an operator.}
\req{FR-PAY-16}{M}{T}{FR-48, NFR-9}{Failed calls to a payment provider shall be retried with exponential backoff and jitter up to a configured maximum, reusing the same idempotency key (\rr{SEC-PAY-07}). After the maximum, the user shall see a clear failure message and no duplicate charge shall result.}
\req{FR-PAY-17}{M}{T}{new}{A seller shall register a payout account before their first payout. An MFS payout account shall be a wallet on the seller's own verified mobile number. A bank account shall be in a name matching the seller's KYC name and shall be checked by an operator.}
\req{FR-PAY-18}{M}{T}{new}{Adding or changing a payout account shall require step-up authentication (\rr{SEC-AUTH-08}) and shall be notified to the seller's verified mobile number and e-mail. No payout shall be sent to a new or changed account until the cooling-off period \cp{CP-29} has passed.}
\req{FR-PAY-19}{M}{T}{new}{After release, the system shall create a payout of the seller's amount to the registered payout account and track it as \texttt{pending}, \texttt{sent} or \texttt{failed}. A failed payout shall be retried and the seller notified. In the academic deliverable, payouts are simulated with a recorded reference, and are labelled as simulated.}
\req{FR-PAY-20}{C}{T}{new}{If a card payment is charged back after release, the system should record it against the order, notify an operator, and recover the amount from the seller's next payouts.}
\req{FR-PAY-21}{S}{T}{new}{The system should give the buyer a receipt for every payment and refund, and give the seller a statement of every settlement and payout.}
\end{reqs}

\section{Delivery and Handover of Physical Items (DLV)}
\label{sec:fr-dlv}
\begin{reqs}
\req{FR-DLV-01}{M}{T}{FR-50}{At checkout, the system shall compute the straight-line distance between the buyer's and the seller's approximate locations.}
\req{FR-DLV-02}{M}{T}{FR-51}{The seller shall choose, per listing, which fulfilment methods are allowed: self-pickup, courier, or both. Within the allowed methods, the system shall recommend self-pickup when the distance is at or below \cp{CP-14}, and courier otherwise. The buyer may choose any allowed method; self-pickup shall add no delivery fee.}
\req{FR-DLV-03}{M}{T}{FR-52}{For self-pickup, the system shall suggest at least one safe public meetup point near the midpoint of the two locations, drawn from a curated list (for example police stations, shopping-mall entrances or bank branches).}
\req{FR-DLV-04}{M}{T}{new}{For self-pickup, either party may propose a meetup point and time in the order view, and the other shall accept it before it is final. The agreed point and time shall be recorded on the order.}
\req{FR-DLV-05}{M}{T}{FR-52}{Payment shall always be made through the platform before the meetup. The system shall not offer cash payment, and the order view shall show both parties that the item is already paid for.}
\req{FR-DLV-06}{M}{T}{new}{For self-pickup, the system shall show the buyer, and only the buyer, a one-time handover code, with an instruction to give it to the seller only after inspecting the item. The seller shall enter the code in the order view; a correct code records the handover as the delivery event. After \cp{CP-45} wrong entries, code entry shall be locked and both parties notified.}
\req{FR-DLV-07}{M}{T}{FR-53}{Before the meetup, the system shall share only approximate locations between buyer and seller. For courier orders, exact pickup and drop-off addresses shall be sent only to the courier provider and shown to each party only for their own address.}
\req{FR-DLV-08}{S}{T}{FR-54}{For courier fulfilment, the system shall calculate the delivery fare from the fare table \cp{CP-43}, using the listing's weight and size classes, the delivery zone and the distance. The fare shall be fixed at checkout.}
\req{FR-DLV-09}{S}{T}{FR-55}{The system shall show the buyer one total price (item plus delivery) without requiring the buyer to choose a courier.}
\req{FR-DLV-10}{S}{T}{FR-56, FR-57}{After payment is confirmed for a courier order, the system shall create a booking with the configured courier provider (or the mock) and track its status as \texttt{booked}, \texttt{picked\_up}, \texttt{in\_transit}, \texttt{delivered}, \texttt{delivery\_failed} or \texttt{returned\_to\_seller}, shown to both parties. The provider's verified \texttt{delivered} status records the delivery event.}
\req{FR-DLV-11}{S}{T}{new}{If the courier has not picked up the item within \cp{CP-16} of payment, the order shall be cancelled and the buyer refunded in full (\rr{FR-PAY-08}). If the item is not delivered within \cp{CP-17} of pickup, the buyer may open a non-delivery dispute.}
\req{FR-DLV-12}{S}{T}{new}{If the courier returns the item to the seller, the system shall cancel the order and refund the buyer in full, including the delivery fee. Any charge from the courier for the failed delivery shall be recorded as a platform cost in the revenue log.}
\req{FR-DLV-13}{S}{T}{FR-58}{On the delivery event of a courier order, the system shall split the delivery fee between courier payout and platform delivery margin \cp{CP-26}, and record both in the settlement record (\rr{FR-PAY-13}).}
\req{FR-DLV-14}{M}{I}{C-3}{Whenever the mock courier is in use, the order view and the documentation shall state that courier bookings are simulated.}
\end{reqs}

\section{Game-Account Escrow (GAM)}
\label{sec:fr-gam}
Game accounts use the order, payment, escrow and dispute requirements of Sections~\ref{sec:fr-pay} and~\ref{sec:fr-dsp}. The requirements below add what a game account needs in place of physical delivery: proof of ownership before listing, a sealed credential handover, a guided takeover and a protection hold (Supplementary Report, Section~5.2; Proposal, Section~1.7).
\begin{reqs}
\req{FR-GAM-01}{M}{T}{C-6}{The game-account feature shall be controlled by the feature switch \cp{CP-36}. When it is off, new game-account listings and purchases shall be refused and active game-account listings hidden. Orders already paid shall continue to completion under these requirements.}
\req{FR-GAM-02}{M}{T}{FR-92}{A verified seller shall be able to create a game-account listing recording: the game, the platform, the in-game player ID, and a description of the account's contents (for example level, items and linked purchases). The listing shall not contain or request any login detail.}
\req{FR-GAM-03}{M}{T}{FR-93}{Before a game-account listing is published, the system shall issue the seller a one-time ownership code. The seller shall place the code in a visible field of the game profile (for example the bio or in-game name) and upload a screenshot showing the code and the player ID. An operator shall approve the listing only after checking both, and shall reject it with a reason otherwise.}
\req{FR-GAM-04}{M}{T}{FR-94}{The system shall allow at most one active game-account listing per game and player ID. It shall refuse a new listing for a player ID whose earlier sale is still in its takeover window or protection period.}
\req{FR-GAM-05}{M}{T}{FR-95}{Every game-account listing shall display a disclosure that the game's publisher may prohibit account transfers and may suspend or ban the account. The disclosure shall state that TrustBazaar's protection covers the handover, the takeover, and recovery by the seller during the protection period, but not action by the publisher. The system shall record the buyer's acknowledgement before accepting payment.}
\req{FR-GAM-06}{M}{T}{new}{A game account may be sold at a fixed price or by auction. No delivery fee applies, and the delivery requirements of Section~\ref{sec:fr-dlv} do not apply.}
\req{FR-GAM-07}{M}{T}{FR-96}{At checkout, the buyer's browser shall generate an encryption key pair using the Web Cryptography API (\rr{SEC-DAT-10}). The private key shall be non-extractable and shall remain only in that browser's storage. Only the public key shall be sent to the server, attached to the order. The buyer shall be told to complete the purchase and takeover in the same browser.}
\req{FR-GAM-08}{S}{T}{new}{The system should show the buyer and the seller a short fingerprint of the buyer's public key, so that they can compare it before the seller encrypts.}
\req{FR-GAM-09}{M}{T}{FR-97}{The seller's browser shall encrypt the login details (login identifier, password and any recovery or backup codes) with the buyer's public key. Only the resulting ciphertext shall be sent to and stored by the server; the plaintext shall never leave the seller's browser.}
\req{FR-GAM-10}{M}{T}{FR-98}{The system shall ask the seller for the credentials only after the payment is \texttt{held}. If the seller does not deliver them within \cp{CP-22}, the order shall be cancelled and the buyer refunded in full (\rr{FR-PAY-08}).}
\req{FR-GAM-11}{M}{T}{FR-98}{The ciphertext shall be retrievable only by the buyer of that order, from an authenticated session. Every retrieval shall be recorded in the credential-access log with time, user and session (\rr{SEC-GAM-02}).}
\req{FR-GAM-12}{S}{T}{new}{If the buyer loses access to the private key (for example by clearing browser data), the buyer may request one re-key during the takeover window. The buyer's browser shall generate a new key pair, and the seller shall be asked to encrypt again. The takeover window shall restart when the new ciphertext is delivered. The re-key shall be logged.}
\req{FR-GAM-13}{M}{T}{FR-99}{The takeover window \cp{CP-23} shall start when the seller's ciphertext is delivered, whether or not the buyer has opened it. When the buyer retrieves the credentials, the system shall present a takeover checklist: change the password; change the recovery e-mail; change the recovery phone; remove linked social logins; and enable two-step verification. The buyer shall confirm each step, and the remaining takeover window shall be shown as a countdown.}
\req{FR-GAM-14}{M}{T}{FR-100}{If, within the takeover window, the buyer reports that the credentials do not work, the system shall open a dispute of type \texttt{credentials\_invalid} (\rr{FR-DSP-01}). Once the buyer has confirmed changing the recovery e-mail, the system shall no longer accept a \texttt{credentials\_invalid} report for that order.}
\req{FR-GAM-15}{M}{T}{new}{The takeover is complete when the buyer confirms every checklist step. If the takeover window ends without the buyer confirming every step or reporting a problem, the takeover shall be treated as complete at the end of the window. The moment of completion is the delivery event for the order.}
\req{FR-GAM-16}{M}{T}{FR-101}{After the takeover is confirmed or treated as complete, the payment shall stay \texttt{held} for the protection period \cp{CP-24}. During this period the buyer may open a dispute of type \texttt{seller\_recovery} with evidence (for example the publisher's security e-mails or screenshots).}
\req{FR-GAM-17}{M}{T}{FR-102}{The payment for a game-account order shall be released when the protection period ends, or earlier if the buyer chooses to release it. An open dispute shall prevent release until an operator decides it. The general auto-release timeout \cp{CP-19} shall not apply to game-account orders.}
\req{FR-GAM-18}{M}{T}{FR-103}{When an operator confirms that a seller recovered a sold account, whether through a dispute during the protection period or a report after release, the system shall do three things. It shall suspend the seller. It shall add the seller's NID blind index to the blacklist, with four-eyes approval (\rr{FR-OPS-05}). And it shall let the operator export the order's evidence (ownership proof, credential-access log, takeover confirmations and dispute history) for a complaint to law enforcement.}
\req{FR-GAM-19}{M}{T}{new}{The system shall delete the ciphertext once the order has reached a terminal escrow state and no dispute is open on it. The credential-access log shall be kept (Table~\ref{tab:retention}).}
\req{FR-GAM-20}{C}{T}{new}{The system may warn a seller who appears to be sending a password or login details in chat, and direct them to the sealed handover instead.}
\end{reqs}

\chapter{Security Requirements}
\label{ch:sec}
TrustBazaar holds identity documents, moves money and holds game-account credentials, so security is part of the product's central claim. This chapter states the security objectives, the threats the system must withstand, and the requirements that follow from them. The baseline is OWASP ASVS 5.0 Level~2 [R3], with NIST SP 800-63B-4 [R5] for authentication and PCI DSS v4.0.1 [R6] for card data. How each control is implemented is a design matter for the SDD.

\section{Security Objectives}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{SO-\arabic*}, nosep, leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Money moves only by the rules}: no actor, including an operator, can release or redirect escrowed funds except by a transition in Table~\ref{tab:escrow}.
    \item \textbf{Identity data stays confidential}: KYC images, NID numbers and selfies are readable only by the owning user and by the operator reviewing them, and only while doing so.
    \item \textbf{Game-account credentials are unreadable to the platform}: the server and its staff never hold them in readable form.
    \item \textbf{Auctions cannot be manipulated}: bid order, end time and the winner are decided by the server and cannot be altered by any client.
    \item \textbf{Accounts cannot be taken over cheaply}: credential stuffing, OTP abuse and session theft are resisted.
    \item \textbf{Every consequential action is attributable}: actions on money, identity, credentials and cases leave a tamper-evident record.
\end{enumerate}

\section{General}
\begin{reqs}
\req{SEC-GEN-01}{M}{A}{new}{The system shall meet the Level~2 requirements of OWASP ASVS 5.0 [R3]. Before each release, an ASVS checklist shall record, for each requirement, whether it is met, not applicable, or deliberately deviated from, with the reason.}
\req{SEC-GEN-02}{M}{A}{new}{A threat model of each feature area shall be recorded in the SDD before that area is implemented, and reviewed when the area changes.}
\req{SEC-GEN-03}{M}{T}{new}{Every security requirement marked T shall have at least one automated test in the CI pipeline.}
\end{reqs}

\section{Authentication (SEC-AUTH)}
\begin{reqs}
\req{SEC-AUTH-01}{M}{T}{FR-2}{Passwords shall be at least 15 characters long, or at least 8 characters for an account with MFA enabled; when a user turns MFA off, a password shorter than 15 characters must be changed. At least 64 characters and all printable Unicode characters, including spaces, shall be accepted. No composition rules, periodic expiry, hints or security questions shall be used [R5].}
\req{SEC-AUTH-02}{M}{T}{new}{New and changed passwords shall be checked against a list of breached and commonly used passwords and against context-specific words (for example ``trustbazaar'' or the user's own name or number), and rejected if found.}
\req{SEC-AUTH-03}{M}{T}{FR-2}{Passwords shall be stored only as salted Argon2id hashes, with at least the parameters OWASP recommends (19~MiB memory, 2 iterations, parallelism 1). bcrypt shall not be used: it ignores input beyond 72 bytes, and a Bangla passphrase of 25 characters can already exceed that. Passwords shall never be logged or stored in plaintext.}
\req{SEC-AUTH-04}{M}{T}{NFR-7}{After \cp{CP-04} failed sign-ins for one account, the system shall delay further attempts progressively, and shall also limit attempts per source address. The account owner shall be notified, and the throttling shall not lock out the legitimate owner permanently.}
\req{SEC-AUTH-05}{M}{T}{new}{Sign-in, registration and password-reset responses shall not reveal whether a mobile number or e-mail is registered. Their response times shall not differ measurably either.}
\req{SEC-AUTH-06}{M}{T}{new}{Registration shall resist enumeration as in \rr{SEC-AUTH-05}: a duplicate number or e-mail shall get the same response as a new one, and the owner of the existing account shall be notified instead.}
\req{SEC-AUTH-07}{M}{T}{new}{MFA shall be mandatory for operator and administrator accounts, using TOTP or a phishing-resistant authenticator (WebAuthn/passkey). SMS shall not be accepted as a staff second factor.}
\req{SEC-AUTH-08}{M}{T}{new}{Step-up authentication (TOTP if enabled, otherwise an OTP to the verified mobile number) shall be required, even in a valid session, for: changing password, mobile number or e-mail; turning MFA off; adding or changing a payout account; releasing a game-account payment early; and closing the account.}
\req{SEC-AUTH-09}{M}{T}{new}{OTPs shall be 6 random digits from a cryptographically secure generator, single-use, valid for \cp{CP-05}, and stored only as hashes. Entry attempts shall be limited per OTP, and sends shall be limited per number and per source address (\cp{CP-05}) to prevent SMS pumping.}
\req{SEC-AUTH-10}{M}{T}{new}{Password-reset tokens shall carry at least 128 bits of randomness, be single-use, expire after \cp{CP-06}, and be stored only as hashes. A successful reset shall end all of the user's sessions and notify the user (\rr{FR-NTF-06}).}
\req{SEC-AUTH-11}{M}{T}{new}{Changing a password shall end every other session of that user.}
\req{SEC-AUTH-12}{S}{T}{new}{The system should recognise devices previously used by an account, and should treat a sign-in from an unrecognised device as new for \rr{FR-NTF-06}.}
\end{reqs}

\section{Session Management (SEC-SES)}
\label{sec:sec-ses}
\begin{reqs}
\req{SEC-SES-01}{M}{T}{FR-3}{Access tokens shall be short-lived (\cp{CP-01}) and signed with an algorithm fixed on the server; tokens with \texttt{alg=none} or an unexpected algorithm shall be rejected.}
\req{SEC-SES-02}{M}{T}{FR-3}{Refresh tokens shall be rotated on every use, subject to an idle limit and an absolute limit (\cp{CP-02}), and stored only as hashes on the server. Reuse of an already rotated refresh token shall revoke the whole session family and notify the user.}
\req{SEC-SES-03}{M}{T}{new}{Session tokens shall be kept in cookies marked \texttt{HttpOnly}, \texttt{Secure} and \texttt{SameSite}, and shall never be placed in browser local storage, in URLs or in logs.}
\req{SEC-SES-04}{M}{T}{new}{Every state-changing request authenticated by cookie shall be protected against cross-site request forgery by an anti-CSRF token or an equivalent origin check, in addition to the \texttt{SameSite} attribute.}
\req{SEC-SES-05}{M}{T}{new}{Sign-out, ``end session'', password change and account suspension shall invalidate the affected sessions on the server immediately; an access token still within its lifetime shall then be refused.}
\req{SEC-SES-06}{M}{T}{new}{Staff sessions shall be subject to the shorter idle and absolute limits in \cp{CP-03}.}
\req{SEC-SES-07}{M}{T}{new}{A WebSocket connection shall be authenticated when it is opened and closed when its session ends or is revoked. Joining an auction room or a chat room shall be authorised on the server: a user may join only chats they participate in, and the order rooms of their own orders.}
\end{reqs}

\section{Access Control (SEC-ACC)}
\begin{reqs}
\req{SEC-ACC-01}{M}{T}{new}{Every request shall be authorised on the server, with access denied by default. Hiding a control in the user interface shall never count as access control.}
\req{SEC-ACC-02}{M}{T}{NFR-5}{Every request that names an object (order, listing, chat, KYC document, dispute, payout account, ciphertext) shall be checked to confirm that the requester is allowed to access that particular object. A user shall not be able to read or change another user's data by altering an identifier.}
\req{SEC-ACC-03}{M}{T}{new}{The roles Guest, Registered user, Verified user, Pro Seller, Operator and Administrator shall have the permissions in Section~\ref{sec:classes} and Section~\ref{sec:fr-ops}, and no others. Role checks shall be made on the server for every protected function.}
\req{SEC-ACC-04}{M}{T}{new}{Request bodies shall be accepted only for fields the endpoint explicitly allows. Fields such as role, KYC status, price after bidding, escrow state or seller payout shall never be settable from client input.}
\req{SEC-ACC-05}{M}{T}{new}{Staff views shall mask personal data by default (for example, phone and NID showing only their last digits), and show the full value only where the task requires it, logging each such display.}
\req{SEC-ACC-06}{M}{T}{FR-5}{KYC images shall be viewable only by the owning user and by an operator assigned to review that submission or a case involving that user. Every view shall be logged with the reason.}
\end{reqs}

\section{Data Protection and Cryptography (SEC-DAT)}
\begin{reqs}
\req{SEC-DAT-01}{M}{T}{new}{All external connections shall use TLS 1.2 or later (TLS 1.3 preferred), with HTTP Strict Transport Security on every response [R9]. Plain HTTP shall only redirect to HTTPS. Connections to providers shall verify certificates.}
\req{SEC-DAT-02}{M}{T}{FR-5, NFR-4}{KYC images and dispute evidence shall be encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM, or an equivalent authenticated cipher, under keys held outside the database. They shall never be reachable through a public or guessable URL.}
\req{SEC-DAT-03}{M}{T}{new}{NID and passport numbers shall be stored encrypted. For the uniqueness check (\rr{FR-KYC-04}) and the blacklist (\rr{FR-TNS-06}), a blind index (HMAC-SHA-256 under a separate secret key) shall be used, so that a copy of the database does not reveal the numbers.}
\req{SEC-DAT-04}{M}{T}{NFR-4}{Protected files shall be served only through short-lived signed URLs (\cp{CP-31}) issued to an authorised viewer after the check in \rr{SEC-ACC-06}.}
\req{SEC-DAT-05}{M}{I}{NFR-8}{Encryption keys, signing secrets and provider credentials shall be kept in environment configuration or a secrets manager, never in source control, container images or logs. Every key shall carry a version so that it can be rotated without losing access to older data.}
\req{SEC-DAT-06}{S}{T}{new}{Backups shall be encrypted, and access to them shall be restricted and logged.}
\req{SEC-DAT-07}{M}{T}{new}{Passwords, OTPs, tokens, NID numbers, payout account numbers and game-account ciphertext shall never be written to application logs, error messages, analytics or URLs. Mobile numbers and e-mail addresses in logs shall be masked.}
\req{SEC-DAT-08}{M}{T}{new}{Metadata (including EXIF location data) shall be removed from every uploaded image before it is stored or shown.}
\req{SEC-DAT-09}{M}{T}{new}{Random values used for security (tokens, codes, keys, identifiers that grant access) shall come from a cryptographically secure generator.}
\req{SEC-DAT-10}{M}{I}{new}{Game-account credential encryption shall use standard Web Cryptography API algorithms, either RSA-OAEP with SHA-256 and a key of at least 3072 bits, or ECDH on P-256 with AES-256-GCM. No other algorithm shall be used and no custom construction written (C-4).}
\req{SEC-DAT-11}{M}{T}{new}{Public identifiers of orders, listings, files and users shall not be sequential or predictable.}
\end{reqs}

\section{Game-Account Credential Security (SEC-GAM)}
\begin{reqs}
\req{SEC-GAM-01}{M}{T}{NFR-21}{Game-account login details shall never be transmitted to or stored on the server in plaintext, or in any form that the server or a staff member can decrypt.}
\req{SEC-GAM-02}{M}{T}{NFR-22}{The credential-access log (\rr{FR-GAM-11}) shall be append-only and shall be kept for the lifetime of the order and of any dispute on it, and then for the period in Table~\ref{tab:retention}.}
\req{SEC-GAM-03}{M}{T}{new}{Credential-entry and credential-view pages shall load no third-party scripts, and shall be protected by the strict content security policy in \rr{SEC-WEB-01}.}
\end{reqs}

\section{Input, Output and File Handling (SEC-INP)}
\begin{reqs}
\req{SEC-INP-01}{M}{T}{new}{Every input shall be validated on the server against an explicit schema (type, length, range, format). Requests with unknown or invalid fields shall be rejected.}
\req{SEC-INP-02}{M}{I}{new}{Database access shall use parameterised queries or the ORM's parameter binding only. Building a query by concatenating user input shall not be allowed, and this shall be checked by static analysis (\rr{SEC-SDL-03}).}
\req{SEC-INP-03}{M}{T}{new}{User-supplied text (listing text, chat, reviews, display names) shall be treated as plain text and encoded for its output context. User-supplied HTML shall never be rendered.}
\req{SEC-INP-04}{M}{T}{new}{Uploads shall be accepted only as JPEG, PNG or WebP, identified by file content and not by name or declared type, within the limits in \cp{CP-33}. Each upload shall be re-encoded, stored under a random name outside any executable path, and served with a fixed content type and \texttt{X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff}. They should also be scanned for malware.}
\req{SEC-INP-05}{M}{T}{new}{The server shall not fetch any web address supplied by a user.}
\req{SEC-INP-06}{M}{T}{new}{Error responses shown to users shall be generic and shall carry a correlation identifier. Stack traces, queries and internal paths shall appear only in protected logs.}
\end{reqs}

\section{Browser and API Hardening (SEC-WEB)}
\begin{reqs}
\req{SEC-WEB-01}{M}{T}{new}{Every page shall be served with a content security policy that forbids inline scripts (nonces or hashes only), restricts script sources to the site and the named payment provider, and sets \texttt{frame-ancestors 'none'}, except on any page a payment provider must frame.}
\req{SEC-WEB-02}{M}{T}{new}{Responses shall set \texttt{X-Content-Type-Options}, a strict \texttt{Referrer-Policy} and a restrictive \texttt{Permissions-Policy}. Camera and geolocation shall be allowed only on the pages that need them.}
\req{SEC-WEB-03}{M}{T}{new}{Cross-origin requests to the API shall be allowed only from the configured web-client origins.}
\req{SEC-WEB-04}{S}{I}{new}{Any third-party script or stylesheet loaded from another origin should carry a Subresource Integrity hash.}
\req{SEC-WEB-05}{M}{T}{new}{The API shall return only the fields each view needs. Internal fields (hashes, blind indexes, other users' contact details, proxy maximums) shall never appear in responses.}
\end{reqs}

\section{Payment Security (SEC-PAY)}
\begin{reqs}
\req{SEC-PAY-01}{M}{I}{FR-49}{Card data shall be entered only on Stripe-hosted pages or Stripe-hosted fields, so that card numbers never reach TrustBazaar's servers. The payment pages shall meet the SAQ A conditions of PCI DSS v4.0.1 [R6], including protection of the page against malicious scripts.}
\req{SEC-PAY-02}{M}{I}{FR-49}{MFS wallet PINs and OTPs shall be entered only on the provider's own pages. TrustBazaar shall never request, receive or store them, or store raw card or wallet credentials; only provider tokens and references shall be stored.}
\req{SEC-PAY-03}{M}{T}{NFR-6}{Every provider webhook or callback shall be accepted only after its signature (or the provider's equivalent verification query) has been checked with the secret for the current environment.}
\req{SEC-PAY-04}{M}{T}{new}{Webhooks shall be rejected if their timestamp is outside \cp{CP-32}, and every event identifier shall be stored, so that a replayed event has no effect (\rr{FR-PAY-04}).}
\req{SEC-PAY-05}{M}{T}{new}{Sandbox and production provider credentials shall be separate. A build configured for sandbox shall refuse production credentials, and the reverse.}
\req{SEC-PAY-06}{M}{T}{new}{Amounts, currency and order references shall be computed on the server; any amount sent by the client shall be ignored.}
\req{SEC-PAY-07}{M}{T}{new}{Every payment, refund and payout request to a provider shall carry an idempotency key unique to that operation, so that a retry can never charge, refund or pay twice.}
\end{reqs}

\section{Abuse Prevention (SEC-ABU)}
\begin{reqs}
\req{SEC-ABU-01}{M}{T}{NFR-7, FR-88}{The system shall rate-limit sign-in, registration, OTP requests, password reset, bidding, chat, listing creation, reporting, search and checkout, per account and per source address, using the limits in \cp{CP-04}, \cp{CP-05} and \cp{CP-35}. A limited request shall receive a clear retry-after response.}
\req{SEC-ABU-02}{S}{T}{new}{The system should present a human-verification challenge after repeated failures or unusual request rates on registration, sign-in and OTP requests.}
\req{SEC-ABU-03}{M}{T}{new}{Rate-limit state shall be shared across backend instances, so that adding instances does not multiply the allowance.}
\end{reqs}

\section{Logging, Audit and Monitoring (SEC-LOG)}
\begin{reqs}
\req{SEC-LOG-01}{M}{T}{FR-90}{The system shall log these security events: sign-in success and failure; MFA changes; password, mobile and e-mail changes; session revocation; OTP failures; every staff action (\rr{FR-OPS-07}); every view of KYC data or case files; every credential retrieval; every bid; every payment, escrow, refund and payout state change; every parameter or feature-switch change; and every rejected escrow transition (\rr{FR-PAY-06}).}
\req{SEC-LOG-02}{M}{T}{FR-90}{The audit log shall be append-only through the application and tamper-evident: each entry shall include a hash of the previous entry, and a verification job shall report any break in the chain.}
\req{SEC-LOG-03}{M}{T}{new}{Log entries shall record UTC time from an NTP-synchronised clock, the actor, the source address and a correlation identifier.}
\req{SEC-LOG-04}{M}{T}{new}{The audit log shall be readable only by administrators (read-only) and by operators for the cases they handle. It shall not be changeable or deletable by any application role.}
\req{SEC-LOG-05}{S}{T}{new}{The system should alert the team on at least these events: a spike in failed sign-ins; OTP send volume above normal; one staff member viewing many KYC records in a short time; a break in the audit-log hash chain; repeated webhook signature failures; and any rejected escrow transition.}
\end{reqs}

\section{Secure Development and Supply Chain (SEC-SDL)}
\label{sec:sec-sdl}
\begin{reqs}
\req{SEC-SDL-01}{M}{I}{new}{The main branch shall be protected. Every change shall be merged by pull request after passing CI and after review by a team member other than the author. Changes to authentication, payment, escrow, credential handling or authorisation shall be reviewed specifically for security.}
\req{SEC-SDL-02}{M}{T}{NFR-16}{The CI pipeline shall run linting, unit tests, the concurrency suite and the security tests on every push and pull request to the main branch.}
\req{SEC-SDL-03}{M}{I}{new}{Static analysis (CodeQL) shall run on every pull request and weekly. A new high-severity finding shall block the merge until it is fixed or documented as a false positive.}
\req{SEC-SDL-04}{M}{T}{new}{Dependencies shall be pinned by lock file and scanned for known vulnerabilities on every pull request (\texttt{npm audit} and Dependabot). The build shall fail on a known critical or high vulnerability with an available fix, unless a documented exception exists.}
\req{SEC-SDL-05}{M}{I}{NFR-8}{Secret scanning with push protection shall be enabled for the repository. Any secret found in history shall be treated as compromised and rotated.}
\req{SEC-SDL-06}{S}{T}{new}{Container images should be scanned for known vulnerabilities before release.}
\req{SEC-SDL-07}{C}{I}{new}{A software bill of materials (SBOM) in a standard format may be produced for each release.}
\req{SEC-SDL-08}{S}{T}{new}{A dynamic scan (OWASP ZAP baseline) should be run against the release candidate, and its findings triaged before the demonstration.}
\req{SEC-SDL-09}{M}{T}{new}{The automated tests shall include negative authorisation tests for each protected endpoint: another user's object; a lower role; no session; and an expired session.}

\req{SEC-INF-05}{M}{T}{new}{Production configuration shall disable debug output and automatic schema synchronisation.}

\section{Performance (PERF)}
Performance targets are measured under the reference load in \rr{NFR-PERF-01}, on the Docker Compose deployment, using a load-testing tool, and reported as the 95th percentile (p95) of server-side response time unless stated otherwise.
\begin{reqs}
\req{NFR-PERF-01}{M}{A}{new}{Reference load: 500 concurrent signed-in users; 200 clients viewing one auction; 20 bids per second on that auction for 60 seconds; 50 simultaneous purchase attempts on the last unit of one fixed-price listing; and a catalogue of 100{,}000 listings.}
\req{NFR-PERF-02}{M}{T}{NFR-1}{A bid shall be accepted or rejected within 500~ms (p95).}
\req{NFR-PERF-03}{M}{T}{NFR-2}{A change in auction state shall reach every connected viewer of that auction within 1~second (p95), measured from acceptance of the bid to receipt by the client.}
\req{NFR-PERF-04}{M}{T}{new}{A chat message shall reach an online recipient within 1~second (p95).}
\req{NFR-PERF-05}{M}{T}{NFR-3}{A filtered search shall return within 2~seconds (p95).}
\req{NFR-PERF-06}{M}{T}{new}{A verified provider confirmation shall move the order to \texttt{held} within 5~seconds of receipt (p95).}
\req{NFR-PERF-07}{S}{T}{new}{On a simulated 4G connection and a mid-range phone profile, the home, listing and checkout pages should reach Largest Contentful Paint within 2.5~seconds.}
\req{NFR-PERF-08}{M}{T}{new}{Under the reference load, the correctness guarantees in Appendix~\ref{app:accept} shall hold without exception: no duplicate highest bid, no over-sale, no duplicate settlement.}
\end{reqs}

\section{Reliability and Availability (REL)}
\begin{reqs}
\req{NFR-REL-01}{M}{T}{NFR-11}{Restarting any single container, including Redis, shall not change any auction's authoritative end time and shall not lose an accepted bid, a confirmed payment or an escrow transition. Authoritative state shall be held durably in PostgreSQL.}
\req{NFR-REL-02}{M}{T}{new}{A bid, purchase reservation, payment confirmation or escrow transition shall be acknowledged only after it has been durably committed.}
\req{NFR-REL-03}{M}{T}{new}{Scheduled actions (auction close, payment-window expiry, checkout-hold expiry, fulfilment deadlines, auto-release, end of protection period, reconciliation) shall be idempotent and shall catch up after downtime. An action that fell due during an outage shall run once on recovery, based on the recorded times, and never twice.}
\req{NFR-REL-04}{S}{T}{new}{When a payment or courier provider is unavailable, the system should refuse new checkouts that depend on it, with a clear message, and leave existing orders unaffected. The payment window of an auction that ends during a provider outage should be extended by the length of the outage.}
\req{NFR-REL-05}{S}{T}{new}{\textbf{(prod)} The database should be backed up daily, with continuous write-ahead-log archiving, for a recovery point objective of 15 minutes and a recovery time objective of 4 hours. A restore should be tested before each release.}
\req{NFR-REL-06}{C}{A}{new}{\textbf{(prod)} Target availability is 99.5\% per calendar month, excluding announced maintenance.}
\end{reqs}

\section{Information the System Shall Keep}
\begin{reqs}

\section{Settlement Example}
The following worked example fixes the fee arithmetic of \rr{FR-PAY-13}, \rr{FR-PAY-14}, \rr{FR-MON-01} and \rr{FR-DLV-13}, and is to be used as a test case. A standard seller sells an item for BDT 10{,}000 with courier delivery at BDT 150. Commission is 5\% and delivery margin is 10\%.
\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}l r@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Line} & \textbf{BDT} \\
\midrule
Collected from buyer & 10{,}150.00 \\
Platform commission (5\% of 10{,}000) & 500.00 \\
Platform delivery margin (10\% of 150) & 15.00 \\
Courier payout (150 $-$ 15) & 135.00 \\
Seller payout (10{,}000 $-$ 500) & 9{,}500.00 \\
\midrule
Check: 500 + 15 + 135 + 9{,}500 & 10{,}150.00 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
With a partial refund of BDT 2{,}000 decided after delivery: the refund is taken from the item price; the commission is 5\% of the BDT 8{,}000 released, which is BDT 400; the seller receives BDT 7{,}600; and the delivery fee is not refunded because the courier had delivered. The parts then total 2{,}000 + 400 + 15 + 135 + 7{,}600 = 10{,}150.

\chapter{Open Issues}
\label{app:open}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{OI-\arabic*}, leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Fee levels.} The commission rates (\cp{CP-25}), delivery margin (\cp{CP-26}) and featured-listing fees (\cp{CP-37}) are placeholders until the team agrees them.
    \item \textbf{Retention periods.} The six-year period for financial records and the periods for KYC data (Table~\ref{tab:retention}) are to be confirmed against Bangladeshi accounting, tax and anti-money-laundering rules and the PDPO, including any rules made under it.
    \item \textbf{PDPO detail.} The PDPO was gazetted on 6 November 2025, with a compliance window reported as 18 months. The deadline for breach notification and the data-localisation categories are to be confirmed from the official text before production use.
    \item \textbf{Payouts in sandbox.} Whether bKash or Nagad sandboxes offer disbursement (payout) APIs to the project is not yet known; until then payouts are simulated (\rr{FR-PAY-19}).
    \item \textbf{Courier sandbox.} Availability of Pathao or Steadfast sandbox access is not yet known; the mock courier is the default (C-3).
    \item \textbf{Liveness check.} Whether the basic randomized-action check (\rr{FR-KYC-02}) is reviewed manually by the operator or by the optional pre-check service (\rr{FR-KYC-11}) is to be decided in design.
    \item \textbf{Game-account legality.} Trading accounts conflicts with most publishers' terms (Supplementary Report, Section~5.2). Legal advice is needed before any production launch of the game-account feature, which is why it can be switched off (\cp{CP-36}).
\end{enumerate}
